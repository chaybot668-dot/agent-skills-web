My Approach to the Market

Six disciplines, one process.

The areas of focus that shape every trading decision.

  1. 01

    Market Analysis

    Building context before any trade: trend, session and the events that move markets.

  2. 02

    Liquidity

    Understanding where orders rest and how price interacts with those areas.

  3. 03

    Market Structure

    Reading highs, lows and ranges to frame direction and invalidation.

  4. 04

    Trading Execution

    Entering only when conditions meet the plan, with risk set before the order.

  5. 05

    Risk Management

    Sizing positions and limiting exposure inside the firm’s rules and my own.

  6. 06

    Performance Review

    Journaling and reviewing trades to refine the system over time.
